Questions

Frequently asked

Who can apply?

Muslim singles between 18 and 40 who are currently unmarried and serious about marriage. Age is verified with a photo ID before filming.

Where is the show filmed?

Episodes are filmed in Dallas, Texas. Singles from anywhere in the United States are welcome to apply; the application asks whether you are able to travel.

May my family or wali attend?

Yes. Walis and family members are welcome on filming day.

How do you keep the show halal?

Modest dress, no physical contact, and no games designed to embarrass anyone. The format is built around finding a spouse, not creating drama.

Where will episodes be shared?

Full episodes are published on YouTube, with clips on TikTok and Instagram. Everyone who appears on camera signs a release before filming.

Does applying guarantee a spot?

No. Our casting team reviews every application and contacts the singles selected for an episode.
