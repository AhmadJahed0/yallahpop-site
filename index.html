FAQ

Questions? We've got you.

Who can apply?

Muslim singles ages 18–40 who are currently single and serious about marriage. We verify age with a photo ID before filming.

Where is it filmed?

Episodes are filmed in Dallas, Texas. Singles from anywhere in the U.S. are welcome to apply — the application asks whether you can travel.

Can my family or wali come?

Yes. Walis and family members are welcome on filming day.

Is it halal?

We keep it respectful: modest dress, no physical contact, and no games. The show is built around finding a spouse, not drama.

Will I be on YouTube and TikTok?

Yes. Episodes are posted on YouTube, and clips on TikTok and Instagram. Everyone on camera signs a release before filming.

Does applying guarantee I'll be on the show?

No. Our casting team reviews every application and contacts the singles who are selected.
